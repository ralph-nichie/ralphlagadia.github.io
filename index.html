Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you get in touch.

How long does a website take?

A one-page site usually takes about 2 to 3 weeks. Larger sites and stores take longer. I’ll give you a clear timeline on our first call.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the pages and features you need. After a free call, you’ll get a fixed quote, so there are no surprise charges later.

What do I need to prepare?

Your business name, logo if you have one, photos, and a short description of what you offer. If you don’t have these yet, we can sort it out together.

Can I edit the website myself?

Yes, if you want to. I can set it up so you can change text, photos, and products on your own, and I’ll show you how.

Will it work on phones?

Yes. Every site is built to work well on phones, tablets, and computers.

Do I own the website?

Yes. After full payment, the website, content, and files belong to you, and your domain and hosting accounts are set up in your name.

What if I need changes after launch?

A period of free fixes is included after launch. For ongoing updates, I can offer a simple maintenance plan.
