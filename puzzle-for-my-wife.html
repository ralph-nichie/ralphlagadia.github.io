For you, my love

Hi baby wifey, you’re everything to me, and my love will never fade or be gone. I will keep our promises to each other and stay together. Even if you feel that this is hopeless, I will remain calm and keep my promise to stay.

We hurt each other sometimes, but that doesn’t mean this is the end for the both of us. It’s part of our growth. We won’t always have just full happiness. Sometimes a little spice like fighting may be needed too, so we can do things we weren’t aware we were capable of, in a good way.

The fights I’m referring to are not cheating or anything that could truly collapse our relationship. I mean the kind where we argue about who should clean the dishes, who should cook, or who should take care of our kids. That’s the kind of argument I mean.
